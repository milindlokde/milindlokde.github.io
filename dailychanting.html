Daily Chanting to reinforce Buddha's Teachings. Use phalanges of your four fingers of right hand to repeat the chants 12 times. Use phalanges of your three or four fingers of left hand to repeat the chants for every 12 counts of right hand. That makes 108 or 144 repetitions. Chose to repeat this process 10 time in single session or 10 times during the day to make it 1080 time or 1440 times each day.
